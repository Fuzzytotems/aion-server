### Proposed corrections (Java behaviour kept)

- `PlayerGroupStats.onRemovePlayer` never clears `minLevelPlayer`/`maxLevelPlayer`, and it recomputes no exp level: a group keeps the level
  span of its last join after the lowest or highest member left (read by `GroupRecruitment` and `SM_CHAT_WINDOW`), and holds the departed
  member's `Player` until the next join. Kept (m5g-plan.md D7).
- `PlayerConnectedEvent` compares the reconnected player with the leader by `AionObject.equals` - **object ids**, not identity (m5g-plan.md
  §2.11 item 8 says no game object overrides `equals`; `AionObject` does, AionObject.java:52-64) - so a leader who relogs within the
  removetime before any other member took over keeps leadership through `changeLeader(new PlayerGroupMember(player))` and the warning
  "leader reconnected, but should have lost leadership on disconnect". Ported as Java has it; a plan correction, not a code one.
