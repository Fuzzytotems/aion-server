## The delayed release of a self-captured base's flag (lane B, P5-12b1, 2026-10-19; the D17 soak)

The D17 soak of 2026-10-18 (`gs.scenario.m5i_soak` on a5c4826ed) failed K3 on `Npc 105518 refcount 1` in census.txt. Five more base flags were
reported 10 minutes after their removal and released later: 105637, 105524, 106308, 105551 and 105603. The leak probe found "no holder".

**This is Java's reachability, kept on purpose (the coordinator's decision of 2026-10-19).** When its map region is inactive, a base's assault
task captures the base with a chance of 20 (Base.java:126-137). `BaseService.capture` stops the Base, despawning its npcs and the flag among them,
and starts a new one. The same task then calls `scheduleAssault()` on the stopped Base, and that task pins the stopped Base for the assault
delay: 7.5 to 20 minutes for a casual base, 30 to 120 on 600100000. All that time the Base holds `flag` and `assaulter`, as Java's does. The task
fires, returns at `isStopped`, and the Base and its npcs go. The census reports the flag when the delay is over 10 minutes, or at shutdown.
Neither clearing `flag` at the stop nor skipping that last `scheduleAssault()` was taken:
- the first can read null where Java reads the stale flag, if a stop from another thread lands inside a running task;
- the second changes the random sequence, since `getAssaultDelay` draws.

What changed is the diagnosis (header request m5b3-leak-h03 for Base, see header-requests.md). The probe now names the holder, for example
`Base.flag of the stopped Base 2120, pinned by the pending task(s) .../Base.cpp:135`. Test: `BaseCaptureLifetimeTest`. After a self-capture,
the old flag's one extra reference is the stopped Base's, the probe attributes it to that Base and its task, and the flag is released once the
stale task has fired.

**Still open:** K3 counts such a flag when a self-capture falls in the last 10 minutes before shutdown, or when the delay passes 10 minutes. A
census excuse for an object held only by a holder that a pending task pins needs `LeakCensus.h` (P4-02b, frozen), and is the owner's decision.
