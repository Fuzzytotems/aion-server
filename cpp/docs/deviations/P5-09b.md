P5-09b's rows from M5c on.

### Java behaviour kept on purpose (pinned by the tests; the fixes are the user's)

- **D7, the double-confirm race** (`ExchangeService.confirmExchange`, ExchangeService.java:225-232; marked `// java-race` in `confirmExchange` and
  `performTrade`). `confirm()` and the partner's `isConfirmed()` are a check-then-act on plain fields, so two `CM_EXCHANGE_OK` processed at once
  can both pass the check. **Measured** on the fixture's `DeterministicExecutor` (B's `CM_EXCHANGE_OK` queued after its `confirm()`, run from the
  exchange log line of A's first put, i.e. after A's `performTrade` removed both players' items and before it stored and cleaned up):
  - whole stacks on both sides (`ExchangeRaceTest.BothPassTheCheckAndTheSecondTradeFailsOnTheRemovedWholeStacks`): B's `performTrade` finds
    its item gone, audits "tried to trade not existing item" and "tried to exploit kinah exchange with partner", and ends both exchanges; A's
    trade completes. The trade happens once and every item id is conserved - what D7 expected, but only for this mix (see the next three);
  - split stacks and kinah (`ExchangeRaceTest.BothPassTheCheckAndSplitStacksAreRemovedTwiceAsInJava`): B's `performTrade` succeeds too. Both
    removals run twice (the split stacks are decreased twice, the kinah is taken twice), each split copy is put only once (the second put of the
    same object is refused by `ItemStorage.putItem`'s `putIfAbsent`), and the kinah is paid twice each way. **Nothing is duplicated, but items
    are destroyed** (30 of 150 potions in the case) and the kinah trade is doubled (A paid 600 and received 200 for an offer of 300 against 100).
  - a split stack against a whole stack (`ExchangeRaceTest.BothPassTheCheckAndASplitCopyAlreadyPutHasItsIdReleasedAsInJava`: A offers 20 of
    his 100 potions, B his whole sword): B's `performTrade` fails on the sword as in the whole-stack case, but its `cleanUpExchanges(true, B,
    A)` (ExchangeService.java:249, :273-274) looks for A's split copy in A's cube, where it no longer is - A's `performTrade` has just put it
    into B's cube - and **releases the object id of that live item**. Every item and all kinah are conserved; the copy lives in B's cube while
    `IDFactory` counts its id as released (quarantined count + 1, `recentlyReleased(copyId)` set). In Java, `IDFactory.release` moves
    `nextMinId` down to the id (IDFactory.java:178-184), so the next `nextId()` hands it out again: two objects share one id, and their `inventory`
    rows can collide. The port's 300 s quarantine and monotone cursor (IDFactory.h) delay that reuse; they do not
    prevent it.
  - a whole stack against a split stack and kinah (`ExchangeRaceTest.BothPassTheCheckAndTheWholeStackSideReleasesTheSplitCopyItIsAboutToGetAsInJava`:
    A offers his whole 100 potions, B 20 of his 50 and 100 kinah): B's `performTrade` takes B's split part and B's kinah a second time, then
    fails on A's potions, and its `cleanUpExchanges(true, B, A)` releases the id of B's split copy, which is still in B's exchange. A's
    `performTrade` then puts that copy into A's cube and pays B's kinah once. B loses 20 potions and 100 kinah that nobody receives, and A holds
    a live item whose id is released, as above.
  - So whole stacks on both sides are the only mix whose cost is an audit line; a split stack on either side destroys items or kinah, or frees
    the id of a live item, or both.
  - **Deviation proposal (D7). The owner chose to fix it on 2026-09-27 (docs/design/owner-decisions.md), as its own commit after M5c stage
    1; until that commit the port keeps Java's behaviour and these cases pin it:** serialize the two confirmations of one pair - a per-pair "trade started" flag that
    `performTrade` sets with a compare-and-set before its first removal and that a second `confirmExchange` finds set (or a monitor around the
    check and `performTrade` per pair). The second `performTrade` then never runs, so none of the four consequences above can happen, the id
    release included. The mutation runs tried the idea with the existing `locked` flag (both exchanges `lock()`ed at the start of
    `performTrade`, a second `performTrade` returning when both are locked): all four race cases fail - the second trade is gone - while the
    sequential cases and the normal trade pass. That exact form is **not** the fix, because a real client locks (`CM_EXCHANGE_LOCK`) before it
    confirms, so a dedicated flag is needed.
- **`performTrade` does not undo a removal** (ExchangeService.java:248-252): when the second removal fails after the first succeeded, the first
  player's whole stacks stay out of the cube (their rows are unchanged, so a relog brings them back), and his split parts and kinah are lost.
  Test: `ExchangeTest.WhenTheOtherPlayersItemIsGoneTheConfirmingPlayersRemovalIsNotUndoneAsInJava`.
- **A refused line of a sale to an npc** (TradeService.java:194-244) returns after the earlier lines were taken and before anything is paid or
  listed for buy-back. Test: `TradeServiceTest.ARefusedLineLeavesTheEarlierLinesSoldUnpaidAsInJava`. (A line the daily sell limit cannot pay
  is different: it ends the list with a `break` (:223-226), so the earlier lines are paid and listed. Test, with the shipped
  `gameserver.limits.enable = true`: `TradeServiceTest.TheDailySellLimitCutsALineAndStopsTheListAtTheFirstLineItCannotPay`.)
- **A private-store stack the seller used up** (PrivateStoreService.java:152-160) stops the sale after the earlier rows moved to the buyer and
  before any kinah moved: those rows are the buyer's unpaid. Test: `PrivateStoreTest.AStackTheSellerUsedUpStopsTheSaleHalfWayAsInJava`.

### Loud on purpose

`AbyssPointsService.addAp` stays `AION_UNPORTED` (m5c-plan.md §2.2 row 5, W: no AP, abyss or reward vendor on the start maps). It is reached by
`performBuyTransaction` when the list needs AP, which is when a bought item's acquisition is of type AP or ABYSS
(`TradeList.calculateAbyssRewardBuyList` adds AP for those two types only, whatever the vendor's type), by `performSellForAPToShop` (the
custom `gameserver.selling.apitems.enabled`) and by `performBuyFromTradeInTrade` (the capital economy, D2). Goods with a REWARD or COUPON
acquisition (the shipped data has 7,742 and 200 that need an item and no AP) cost only their tokens and never reach it. Tests:
`TradeServiceTest.AnAbyssVendorWantsApAndMedalsAndReachesTheUnportedApArm` (adetes 203386 and his shield of goods list 535, verbatim rows) and,
for the silent token path, `TradeServiceTest.ARewardVendorTakesTheTokensAndGivesTheGoodsWithoutAp` (the REWARD vendor gintarunerk 801517,
goods list 1770, Pandarunerk's Delve Scroll for 2 Ancient Coins each, verbatim rows).

### Not covered by a test, named

- `canBuyLimitItem` and the buy and sell limit updates of a limited item (no merchant of the start maps sells one; `LimitedItemTradeService.start`
  needs goods with a sales time).
- `performSellForAPToShop`'s AP arm and the trade-in past its first refusals (both the capital economy's, D2), `addItem`'s legion-tradeable
  arm (legions: M5h).
- `putItemToInventory`'s equipment-slot reset and unpacking, and `sellStoreItem`'s unpacking. (The id releases are covered:
  `removeItemsFromInventory`'s by `ExchangeTest.ASplitOfferGrownToTheWholeStackMovesTheStackAndReleasesTheCopysId`, `cleanUpExchanges(true)`'s
  by `ExchangeTest.CancelShowsTheOfferedItemsAgainAndTellsThePartner` and the two mixed D7 cases, and the absence of one after a completed
  trade by `ExchangeTest.TheSecondConfirmationTradesAndCleansUp`.)
- `createStoreWithItems`' `RecallService.cancel(player, CANCELLED)` (PrivateStoreService.java:48): nothing in the tree can give a player a
  pending summon request (`RecallService::requestSummon` is `AION_UNPORTED`, the `requests` map is private), so the call returns at once in
  every in-process arrangement and deleting it changes nothing a test can see (tests/playersvc/TeleportStatementsTest.cpp notes the same for
  `TeleportService.abortPlayerActions`). It needs `requestSummon` ported.
- W-28's refusal sites live in other chunks' files (`ItemMoveService`, `ItemSplitService`: P5-07; `CM_SHOW_DIALOG`, `CM_DIALOG_SELECT`,
  `CM_QUESTION_RESPONSE`: P5-15/P5-16; `PlayerRestrictions.canTrade`: P5-13). `TradingRefusalsTest` shows each refusal and its control after the
  cancel; its mutation evidence is a `registerExchange` that registers nothing (all six cases fail), not a mutant of those files.
