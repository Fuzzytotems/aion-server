## M5b-3, stage 1 (player-side lane, m5b3-plan.md P-04)

The soul-bind accept of `Equipment` (Equipment.java:726-764, `Equipment$1.acceptRequest` with its `ItemUseObserver` `Equipment$2` and its 5 s
`Runnable` `Equipment$3`) is ported: `cancelUseItem`, the 5 s animation, the observer (`Equipment_ItemUseObserver`, cycles.toml
`Equipment$2#item` / `#responder`), and the `ITEM_USE` task that removes the observer, sends the closing animation and
`STR_SOUL_BOUND_ITEM_SUCCEED`, binds the item, resends its info, equips it and broadcasts the appearance. The task is a lambda pinned to what
`Equipment$3` captures (the responder, the observer, the item, the Equipment - a part, whose pin retains the owner), the form of
`ItemSocketService`'s godstone task; `Equipment.cpp` no longer has an `AION_UNPORTED`.

| Area | Java | C++ | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `equip(slot, item)` in `Equipment$3.run` | the anonymous class calls its enclosing instance's private `equip` | `Equipment::soulBindItem` - a member, allowed to name the private function - hands `&Equipment::equip` to `Equipment_RequestResponseHandler` (a C++-only member `equipMethod`, a member-function pointer that retains nothing, `// fieldmap:` waiver), which hands it to the task; the task calls `(equipment.*equipMethod)(slot, item)` | The callback structs are no members of `Equipment` and `Equipment.h` declares no friend for them. **Header request m5b3-p04-1** (lane report): `friend struct Equipment_Runnable;` in `Equipment.h` (the `IdianStone_ActionObserver` precedent) would let the task call `equip` directly and remove the member |
| `ItemPacketService.updateItemAfterInfoChange(owner, item)` in `Equipment$3.run` | the Equipment's private `owner` | `responder` | The same object: `soulBindItem` puts the request on the owner's own `ResponseRequester` (`equipItem`, its only caller, passes `owner`), and `ResponseRequester.respond` answers with that requester's player (ResponseRequester.java:42-49). Reading `owner` needs the same private access as `equip` (m5b3-p04-1) |
