## M5b-3, stage 1 (player-side lane, m5b3-plan.md P-04)

The soul-bind accept of `Equipment` (Equipment.java:726-764, `Equipment$1.acceptRequest` with its `ItemUseObserver` `Equipment$2` and its 5 s
`Runnable` `Equipment$3`) is ported: `cancelUseItem`, the 5 s animation, the observer (`Equipment_ItemUseObserver`, cycles.toml
`Equipment$2#item` / `#responder`), and the `ITEM_USE` task that removes the observer, sends the closing animation and
`STR_SOUL_BOUND_ITEM_SUCCEED`, binds the item, resends its info, equips it and broadcasts the appearance. The task is a lambda pinned to what
`Equipment$3` captures (the responder, the observer, the item, the Equipment - a part, whose pin retains the owner), the form of
`ItemSocketService`'s godstone task; `Equipment.cpp` no longer has an `AION_UNPORTED`.

## Fix 2026-10-03: registerExpirable of the title, emotion, motion and pet lists (owner's bug report)

`TitleList`, `EmotionList`, `MotionList` and `PetList` each kept a local `registerExpirable` stub that was `AION_UNPORTED` ("the task
manager (P5-14) has no C++ header yet"), written before `taskmanager/tasks/ExpireTimerTask` was ported. The stubs now forward to
`ExpireTimerTask::getInstance().registerExpirable`, as Java does (TitleList.java:58, EmotionList.java:39, MotionList.java:52,
PetList.java:47). **The bug it caused:** `TitleList.addTitle` threw after `titles.put` and before `storeTitles`, inside
`QuestService.finishQuest`'s `giveReward` - after the items and the exp were paid and before `setStatus(COMPLETE)`. The quest stayed in
REWARD, so the owner turned in quest 1002 "Request of the Elim" twice (the second time the title was "already learned" and the quest
completed); the title was never stored. The same throw met any new emotion or motion, and `PetList.loadPets` for a player with pets.
**Test:** `PlayerDaoTest.ANewTitleEmotionOrMotionIsRegisteredForExpiryAndStored` (DB-backed: a quest-reward title, a timed title, an
already learned one, an emotion and a motion stored); it fails at the old stub (`TitleList.cpp:28`).
