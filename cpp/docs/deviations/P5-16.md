### Not ported on purpose (m5b2-plan.md P-03, **W**)

`CM_TOGGLE_SKILL_DEACTIVATE` and `CM_USE_CHARGE_SKILL` have no C++ file and stay unregistered, because their paths are not ported and no
level-1..10 skill of a starting class is a toggle, a stance or a charge skill:

- `CM_TOGGLE_SKILL_DEACTIVATE` (43 Java lines) needs `EffectController::removeEffect(int32_t)` (`AION_UNPORTED`, K-02) - called once
  directly and once more through `PlayerController::stopStance` - and, to have anything to deactivate, a toggle or stance skill that
  `Skill::useSkill` (S-02) started. Everything else on its path is ported: `SkillData::getSkillTemplate`, `SkillTemplate::isToggle`/`isStance`,
  `AuditLogger::log`, `PlayerController::getStanceSkillId` and `stopStance`.
- `CM_USE_CHARGE_SKILL` (32 Java lines) needs `standins::chargeSkillGetAndUse` gone (m5b2-plan.md P-04, second half), i.e.
  `SkillEngine::getChargeSkill` (`AION_UNPORTED`, S-01) and `ChargeSkill::useSkill` (S-07), and a charge skill that is casting, i.e.
  `Skill::useSkill` (S-02). `Player::getCastingSkill`, `SkillTemplate::isCharge`, `Skill::getCastStartTime` and `CreatureController::useChargeSkill`
  up to the stand-in are ported.
