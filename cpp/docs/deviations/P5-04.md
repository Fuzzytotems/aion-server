  skill has: 620 Armor of Attrition's 8929 is a `<prochealinstant>` (`ProcHealInstantEffect::calculate`, `ProcHealInstantEffect.cpp:7`,
  P5-04, outside §2.4's subset and D13: `AION_UNPORTED`), so an attack on a Gladiator under 620 throws from the observer. The npc provokers of
  the start maps (§2.4(b)) were not traced here.

  post-spawn skill ids are provokers, both `is_post_spawn` on npcs of `npc_skills/instances/301390000_Drakenspire_Depths.xml` (the `IDSEAL_TWIN`

  Only inside Drakenspire Depths; the other post-spawn effect positions (statup, hide, shield, sanctuary, reflector, alwaysresist, heal, statdown,

- `WeaponDualEffect.hasDualWieldEffect` is now answered for `Equipment.equipItem` and `Equipment.checkDualWieldRestriction`, which reached its

and is set before `EffectTemplate.calculate(effect, null, null, element)`, whose answer is not read (a resisted MP attack keeps its reserve:
`ProcEffectsTest.AResistedMpAttackKeepsTheReserveItSetFirst`). Its `element` argument has no observable effect: the four-argument
