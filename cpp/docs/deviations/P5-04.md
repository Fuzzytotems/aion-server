**No deviation elsewhere.** Every other body is its Java method statement by statement: the int arithmetic of `ShieldEffect`,
`ReflectorEffect` and `WeaponDualEffect` (`hitvalue + hitdelta * level`, `maxDamageChance + level * maxDamageDelta`, `value + level * delta`) wraps
through `uint32_t`, `ProcVPHealInstantEffect`'s `long` products through `uint64_t`, and its `(int) (long * int * 0.001)` uses the saturating
`JavaFloat::doubleToInt`. `WeaponMasteryEffect`'s `change == null` is `change.empty()` (JAXB leaves an absent list null, the bound list is empty:
the precedent of `Effect.endEffects`); the mastery lists hold the `Ref`s until `CreatureGameStats.addEffect` has taken its own.

### Reachable after part 3 (unported bodies these classes reach)

- `ReturnEffect.applyEffect` -> `TeleportService.moveToBindLocation`: a bind point on another map (or another instance) takes the cross-map arm
  of `TeleportService.teleportTo`, which reaches `InstanceService::onLeaveInstance` (`services/instance/InstanceService.cpp:170`, P5-13,
  `AION_UNPORTED`). Return to a bind point on the caster's own map is ported end to end (`OtherEffectsTest.ReturnTakesTheCasterToItsBindPoint`).
- `ProvokerEffect$1` applies its `skill_id` through `SkillEngine.applyEffectDirectly`, so a provoker reaches whatever effect class the provoked
  skill has: 620 Armor of Attrition's 8929 is a `<prochealinstant>` (`ProcHealInstantEffect::calculate`, `ProcHealInstantEffect.cpp:7`,
  P5-04, outside §2.4's subset and D13: `AION_UNPORTED`), so an attack on a Gladiator under 620 throws from the observer. The npc provokers of
  the start maps (§2.4(b)) were not traced here.
- The post-spawn provokers of the D11 holdback (`NpcSkillList::getPostSpawnSkills`, `model/skill/NpcSkillList.cpp:91`): two of the 33
  post-spawn skill ids are provokers, both `is_post_spawn` on npcs of `npc_skills/instances/301390000_Drakenspire_Depths.xml` (the `IDSEAL_TWIN`
  pair). The part 3 Regate removed that partial, so these npcs cast them at spawn, and an attack on them runs `ProvokerEffect$1` ->
  `SkillEngine.applyEffectDirectly` into an unported effect class, whose exception comes out of the attack's `notifyAttackedObservers`:
  - 21641 Binding Curse (`<provoker hittype="PHHIT" provoke_target="OPPONENT">`) provokes 20771 Bind, a `<bind>`: `BindEffect::calculate`
    (`skillengine/effect/BindEffect.cpp:11`, P5-04, `AION_UNPORTED`), for every auto attack or PHYSICAL skill on the npc.
  - 21642 Silence Curse (`<provoker hittype="MAHIT" provoke_target="OPPONENT">`) provokes 20772 Silence, a `<silence>`:
    `SilenceEffect::calculate` (`skillengine/effect/SilenceEffect.cpp:11`, P5-04, `AION_UNPORTED`), for every MAGICAL skill on the npc.
  Only inside Drakenspire Depths; the other post-spawn effect positions (statup, hide, shield, sanctuary, reflector, alwaysresist, heal, statdown,
  spellatkinstant) are all in the ported subset.
- `WeaponDualEffect.hasDualWieldEffect` is now answered for `Equipment.equipItem` and `Equipment.checkDualWieldRestriction`, which reached its
  `AION_UNPORTED` before for every one-handed weapon.
- The test paths of every class (including the gate's 1328, 37, 39, 43, 140, 8291 and D13's 8217, 8218, 8751) reach no `AION_UNPORTED` site:
  the fixture's `TearDown` asserts `runtime::unportedHitCount() == 0` for every case.
