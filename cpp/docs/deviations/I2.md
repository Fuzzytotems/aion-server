| Area | Java | C++ | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymous `Runnable`s and `Consumer` | anonymous classes capturing `this`, the player or the npc | lambdas; the captured player and npc as `Ref` (fieldmap's captures) | the runtime's pinning rule |

**Not unit-tested.** These paths are left out:
