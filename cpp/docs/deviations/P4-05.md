## Small tasks 2026-09-30 (branch `fix/small-quick-4`): the GM login arm (m5j-plan.md I-02)

`GMService::onPlayerLogin`'s staff arm is ported (GMService.java:50-56, statement by statement): every entry of
`gameserver.administration.login.execute_commands` goes to `ChatProcessor::getInstance().handleChatCommand(player, cmd)` (ported, P5-14), then
the staff registration and `scheduleBroadcastLogin`, as before. The arm was `AION_UNPORTED` whenever the list was not empty, i.e. with the
shipped `//invis, //invul, //enemy none, //see`, and the throw came before `staffMembers.put`, so PlayerEnterWorldService's catch
(`PlayerEnterWorldService.cpp:344-351`) sent `SM_ENTER_WORLD_CHECK(CONNECTION_ERROR)`: a GM account could not enter the world. **No
behavioural deviation**; the list is read through a snapshot kept in a local (`ConfigValue.h`'s rule for a range-for).

**What a GM does not get yet (not ported, bigger than this task):** the effects of the four login commands - Invis (the HIDE visual state and
its system message), Invul (invulnerability and its message), Enemy `none` (neutral to everyone) and See (sees hidden objects). Their classes
(`game-server/data/handlers/admincommands/{Invis,Invul,Enemy,See}.java`) have no C++ file, and the command framework they need is
`AION_UNPORTED` (m5j-plan.md K-01: `ChatCommand::run`, `toErrorMessage`, `sendInfo`, `join`, `name`, `worldName`, `info`;
`AdminCommand::validateAccess`, `process`; the `PlayerCommand` and `ConsoleCommand` pairs: 13 bodies, P5-14). The C++ `ChatProcessor`
registers no command ("Loaded 0 commands."), so each `handleChatCommand` returns false, as Java's does for an alias it does not know; a GM
enters the world visible, vulnerable and with the normal enmity until M5j stage 0 ports them. The staff `VERSION_INFO` line
(`PlayerEnterWorldService.cpp:527`, `AION_PARTIAL`, K-02, access level 9 by default) is unchanged.

Tests (`tests/cm_ak/GMServiceLoginTest.cpp`, a real Player and AionConnection in the state PlayerEnterWorldService leaves him at
PlayerEnterWorldService.java:323, the shipped values of the three keys the arm reads): the shipped commands with none registered complete
(no throw, no unported hit), register the staff member and send "Your login will be announced in 15s. ..." and, 15 s later, "Your login has
been announced."; with four recording stand-ins registered under the aliases, the calls are `//invis`, `//invul`, `//enemy` with `{"none"}`
and `//see`, in that order, for the GM, and the announcement reaches the other player at 15,000 ms, not at 14,999; a normal account runs no
command, is not registered, receives nothing and schedules nothing. Mutation proof (`AION_SQ4_GM_MUT`, 13 mutants in `GMService.cpp`, one
build; restored, sha256-checked, rebuilt, no mutant string left): the old `AION_UNPORTED` arm (:199, :201, :202, :203, :205 and the second
case), no loop, reversed order, only the first command (:221), the loop for a normal account (:243), a normal account registered (:244), no
staff registration (:202, :222), no announcement (:203, :205, :223, :227, :230, :232), an announcement for a normal account (:245, :246,
:248), the announcement at once (:224, :228, :230), at 14,999 ms (:227, :228), to staff only (:230), "has not been announced" (:205, :230,
