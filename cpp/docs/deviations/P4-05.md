## M5c stage 0 (prereqs lane, m5c-plan.md P-02's `getSellLimit` half, W-26)

`model::getSellLimit(Player&)` (`SellLimitInfo.cpp`) is ported: the band of `Account.getMaxPlayerLevel` gives the limit, then
`Rates.SELL_LIMIT.calcResult(player, limit)` through the existing `RatesInfo.h` (the long overload; SellLimit.java:29-37, Rates.java:143-149).
The site's stale comment (it waited for a `Rates` companion that exists) is gone. **No behavioural deviation**; the float product rounds as
Java's (levels 61-65 give 17150048, not 17150047). The only caller, `PlayerLimitService::updateSellLimit`, stays unported until stage 1 (P-02).
