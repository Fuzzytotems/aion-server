### Reached next (unported bodies the ported classes now reach)

- **A player's hide stops in `PlayerController.onHide`**: it calls `DuelService.fixTeamVisibility`, `AION_UNPORTED` at `services/DuelService.cpp:45`
  (P5-08). `HideEffect.startEffect` has by then added the SPEED function, set the HIDE abnormal state and the visual state, sent SM_PLAYER_STATE and
  scheduled `removeTargetFrom`; the player's three observers (`HideEffect$1` to `$3`) and the cancel on damage come after `onHide` and are not
  reached, and the exception leaves the effect in the controller without an end task. An npc's hide (the post-spawn hides of D7) does not call
  it and runs to the end. Test: `BuffEffectsTest.HideOfAPlayerSetsItsStatesUntilPlayerControllerOnHideStops`.
  **Regate blocker.** This is worse than the throw it replaces: before this part `HideEffect::applyEffect` threw before it changed anything, and
  now a player's hide (3222 *Stealth*, a Scout's level-5 skill of §2.4) leaves the player slowed by 40 %, invisible and in the HIDE state with
  nothing that can end it - no end task, no observer, no cancel on damage - until the effect is dispelled or the player logs out. It cannot be
  closed inside P5-03: Java calls `onHide` before it adds the observers (HideEffect.java:74-118), and the port keeps that order. It closes when
  P5-08 ports `DuelService.fixTeamVisibility` (DuelService.java:176-184, eight lines; under an I-03 lease if P5-08 has no lane open) before the
  regate; the test above then has to cover `HideEffect$1` to `$3` and the cancel on damage.
  **Closed (2026-09-23, after HEAD 760e8ab5c):** P5-08 ported `DuelService.fixTeamVisibility` (docs/deviations/P5-08.md). A player's hide now
  runs `startEffect` to its end in Java order and ends at its duration, at the hider's attack (`HideEffect$2`), at a skill as `HideEffect$1`
  decides, at an item use as `HideEffect$3` decides, and for type 0 at the first hit or damage over time; the end removes the HIDE state, the
  visual state and the SPEED function. The stop-point test is replaced by `BuffEffectsTest.HideOfAPlayerLastsItsDurationAndEndsWithItsStatesAndStatFunction`,
