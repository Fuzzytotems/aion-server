### PvpService and AbyssService, the player-death path (M5b-1)

`PlayerController::onDie` calls `PvpService::doReward(player)` unconditionally (Java PlayerController.java:323). It was unreachable until M5b-1
registered the AI and ported the damage path: no npc could land a hit, so no monster could kill a character. Ported in the stage-1 fixer round.
