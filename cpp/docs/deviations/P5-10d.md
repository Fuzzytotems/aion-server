### Proposed corrections (Java behaviour kept)

1. **`LeagueChangeLeaderEvent` swaps no positions** (LeagueChangeLeaderEvent.java:30-37): it reads `league.getMember(team.getObjectId())`, and
   `team` is the new leader's own alliance, so that alliance gets its old position back and the previous leader's alliance keeps 0. The new
   league leader's alliance is not at position 0. Pinned by `AnAllianceLeaderTakesTheLeagueLead`.
2. **A leader's alliance off position 0 makes `reorganize` throw** (League.java:94-108): `reorganize` calls `changeLeader` on the first alliance
   of the sorted list when its position is above 0; if that alliance already leads (after `LeagueMoveEvent` moved it, or after correction 1's
   no-swap), `GeneralTeam.changeLeader` throws IllegalArgumentException ("... is already the team leader"). Whether a leave or a disband meets
   it depends on the team iteration order. The tests and the gate move the alliances back before any leave.
3. **`LeagueChangeLeaderEvent` for the alliance that already leads throws** the same IllegalArgumentException (`league.changeLeader` on the
   current leader), e.g. CM_PLAYER_STATUS_INFO 32 naming the league leader's own alliance.
