# Header requests after the spine freeze

The integrator's log of header requests (docs/design/hub-headers.md §14): every request a chunk filed against a frozen header, a header of
another chunk, the chunk manifest or the concurrency decisions, with the decision and its reason. Approved changes are applied by the integrator
in all affected files (headers, stubs, the requesting lanes' workarounds, call sites, `fieldmap.toml`, `chunks.cmake`) in one batch.

Decisions: **approved** (applied as requested), **approved (modified)** (applied in a different or smaller form, the reason says how),
**rejected** (not applied; the reason says what happens instead).
